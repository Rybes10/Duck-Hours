ABOUT DUCK HOURS

A fresh perspective
on your code.

Duck Hours is an AI-assisted debugging website that helps programmers understand what went wrong—and why. Describe what your code should do, share your attempt, and work through problems with explanations, highlighted issues, and test cases that compare expected results with actual behavior.

WHY “DUCK HOURS”?

Explain it slowly. See it clearly.

Our name combines office hours with rubber duck debugging, a problem-solving technique where you explain your code aloud, line by line, to a rubber duck—as though you were teaching someone who knows nothing about programming.

Explaining each step encourages you to slow down, question your assumptions, and notice details you might otherwise overlook. Often, describing the problem helps you discover the solution yourself.

YOUR DIGITAL RUBBER DUCK

Understand the problem, not just the fix.

Duck Hours acts as that rubber duck, offering focused prompts and guidance through the debugging workspace. It helps you connect what you intended with what your code actually does, so you can understand the problem rather than simply copy a fix.

Our goal is to help you become a more confident, independent problem solver—one explanation at a time.
